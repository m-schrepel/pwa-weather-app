How the light works

Sun angles use latitude, longitude, date and UTC time, following NOAA solar equations. Sunrise and sunset use the conventional −0.833° solar-center horizon. Times use America/Los_Angeles, including daylight saving.

Brightness, warmth and the blend between day and night paintings are artistic curves driven by sun elevation, not measured illuminance. The horizon glow follows the sun’s compass bearing in an illustrative west-facing view; no sun disc is drawn. The painting’s camera calibration, terrain occlusion and weather are not modeled.
