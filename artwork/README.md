# Watercolor weather artwork

Two original 1024 × 1536 Pacific Northwest lake illustrations were generated using the built-in image generation tool, then optimized as JPEGs in `assets/landscape/`. The approved original project concept images were the editing references; exact prompts are recorded in `prompts.json`. Their skies are intentionally empty so the app can compose weather independently.

The setting is illustrative: evergreen foothills, mossy rocks, fern fronds, broad-leaved evergreen shrubs, a modest landing and driftwood. It is not a depiction of a measured viewpoint or observed ground conditions. Original transparent SVG weather layers are generated by `scripts/generate-backgrounds.mjs`; the matching fourteen weather symbols are in `weather-icons.mjs`.

The raster art uses watercolor washes, pencil contours and warm paper. Sky objects and precipitation remain lightweight code-native shapes with subtle washes. The two canvases and all sixty weather layers total less than 1 MB. Rendering is static, with no animation, flashing lightning, SVG filters or remote dependencies.
